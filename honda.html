What Usually Happens

Most people think ignition suddenly failed.

But a lot of the time, it starts with the key.

After years of use, key wears down.

It stops matching ignition the way it should.

Inside the ignition, small parts have to line up perfectly.

When they don't:

  • • The key gets tight
  • • It starts sticking
  • • Eventually, it won't turn
