Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions.

Do I need a tow truck for the service? ▾
No. Everything is done on the spot. We come to your location in Austin and solve the problem right there — at home, work, parking lot or gas station.
Can you make a key if I lost the only one? ▾
Yes. In many cases we can create and program a new key on the spot, even if no key is available. The process takes approximately 20–40 minutes.
Can you repair the ignition? ▾
In many cases yes. Many ignitions that seem to need replacement can be repaired. We diagnose first and solve the real problem without overspending.
How long does it take to arrive? ▾
We normally arrive in 20–40 minutes depending on your location in the Austin area.
What happens if I ignore the key or ignition problem? ▾
It generally gets worse. A small problem like a stiff key can end with the ignition completely blocked, leaving you stranded. It's better to address it before it fails completely.
Why does my Nissan/Hyundai/Kia say "Key not detected"? ▾
Generally it's due to a low key battery or signal problem. Try bringing the key directly to the start button. If it persists, call us.
Can I start the car if the key battery is dead? ▾
Yes — in many modern vehicles you can bring the key directly to the start button. The car can detect it via internal antenna even with a low battery.
Does the ECU always need to be replaced in Toyota? ▾
No — many times the system can be reset and new keys reprogrammed without replacing the computer. This can save you a lot of money.
Do you work with all car brands? ▾
Yes. We work with Honda/Acura, Toyota/Lexus, Nissan/Infiniti, Ford/Lincoln, Hyundai/Kia, Chevrolet/GMC and Dodge/Chrysler/Jeep, among others.
Why won't my Honda/Ford key turn? ▾
Generally due to key wear. Over time the key loses its shape and no longer matches the ignition correctly. In many cases it can be repaired without replacing the ignition.
